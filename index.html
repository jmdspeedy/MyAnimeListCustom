See the theme in context

Built to feel like a page from a summer journal.

The visual language is playful, but the hierarchy stays practical: artwork frames the list while title, score, progress, status, search, filters, and owner actions remain easy to find.

Why it works

Decorative, without getting in the way.

Native first

MAL links, filters, editing, score, progress, and streaming controls stay functional.

Equal by default

No entry is arbitrarily featured; variation comes from framing and paper details.

Keyboard aware

Hover details also open through focus, with visible ocean-blue focus rings.

Fallback ready

The layout still reads clearly if remote artwork or optional fonts fail to load.

Ready to install

Bring the voyage to your list.

  1. Open the Modern editor. Use MyAnimeList's theme editor.
  2. Paste the full file. Copy the contents of aqours-journal.css.
  3. Save and check both views. Verify the public list and signed-in owner view.
